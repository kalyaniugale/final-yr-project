Privacy Policy

This application is a university final-year project for demonstrating a street-vendor location recommendation system.

Information We Process

The application may process WhatsApp messages and user-selected inputs such as business category, preferred area, and selected recommendation options in order to provide location recommendations.

Use of Information

The information is used only to operate the recommendation demo, generate responses, and support academic evaluation of the project.

Data Storage

Conversation state may be stored temporarily for the purpose of maintaining the current session. The system is not intended to collect unnecessary personal information.

Third-Party Services

The application uses services such as WhatsApp Cloud API and mapping or geospatial services where required for the demonstration.

Important Notice

Recommendations are for decision-support and demonstration purposes only. They do not constitute official NMC allocation, permission, or a guarantee of business success.

Contact

For questions regarding this project, contact the project team through the repository associated with this application.
